Life as a Computer Engineer

What is engineering?


  1. The branch of science and technology concerned with the design, building, and use of engines, machines, and structures.
  2. The action of working artfully to bring something about.

Why I am a computer engineer?


Previously, I was majoring in computer science, so I found being a computer engineer would be a good major to transition to. One of the main causes for the transition from computer science to computer engineering is also from my friend's advice to become a computer engineer. I also like how much the salary of a computer engineer is, which is about six figures! Additionally, I would want to work with computers as an engineer and develop new applications and devices. Another reason I chose computer engineering would be because I would be following in my brother's footsteps as a software engineer.


What are some projects you have worked on?


Some of the many things I have created in the past as a project would be a phone application that would send a mass text to a group of people, a digital stopwatch using only integrated circuits (ICs), and more recently a Minecraft mod implementing archer zombies and mining zombies. I additionally worked on developing a startup for legal tech (CaseViaUs). Right now, I'm working on setting up Arch Linux on my Macbook Pro (2011). More info can be found on my resumes.
